# The Snapshot: 2026
If you are reading this, you have found the digital home for my progress, my projects, and I imagine the occasional technical headache. Right now, I’m a junior at UT San Aantonio, balancing a double major in Cybersecurity and Information Systems while working as an AV Student Manager in the Student Union, and leading the PR team for Rowdy CyberCon. It’s a lot of moving parts, but that is one reason I am making this blog. I want to create a place where I can collect all of my previous experiences into one place because I have realized that doing the work is only half of the battle. Documenting the process is what actually makes the knowledge stick, plus I have somewhere to reference back to later. This website is not meant to be a polished manuel or guide, it is a look into my progress from a student to a cybersecurity professional, and I am starting with where I am at today.

My imediate pathway is pretty packed. I am currently sprinting to prepare for our April Conference, prepping for my Security+ exam, working on this blog, and looking ahead to career goals and masters programs. But one place I know I want to get to is a career in public service. My background as an Eagle Scout has driven my interest in that kind of work, and my goal is to apply my technical skills to defend our national interests. I am writing this this first blog post as a sort of time capsule so I can track how my goals evolve over time. I do not have every technical answer yet, but as I progress with my homelab and class assignments, I plan to use this space to log what I learn.
